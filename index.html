About Me

I am a PhD scholar in Computational Biology at The Institute of Mathematical Sciences (IMSc), Chennai. My research involves the collection, curation, integration and analysis of biological and chemical data.

My doctoral research is broadly focused on cheminformatics and chemical spaces related to human health. I am particularly interested in developing computational resources and using computational methods to derive biological insights from chemical and biomedical data.

I am interested in applying AI and machine learning to biological datasets, combining data-driven approaches with knowledge-graph-based modelling to uncover hidden relationships and generate new biological insights.

Research Interests

Cheminformatics

Chemical space analysis, molecular similarity, activity-cliff prediction, structure-based analysis, natural product chemical spaces and chemical databases.

Computational Biology

Integrative analysis of chemical, biological and biomedical data to understand biological systems.

Drug Discovery

Computational approaches to discover novel chemical scaffolds from natural product chemical space with potential as lead compounds, and to investigate drug–target relationships and underlying therapeutic mechanisms.

Artificial Intelligence

Exploring machine learning, knowledge graphs and data-driven approaches for biomedical research.
